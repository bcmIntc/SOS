Bounce-slot availability probes (OFI only)
=========================================

Enable with SHMEM_OFI_BOUNCE_POOL_STATS=1. The default is off.
This does not change completion flags, pool size, or admission rules.

Every PE reports one summary for each destroyed context, including zero-wait
contexts. has_pool distinguishes unused/disabled pools from active pools.
Unlike OFI_PUT_BATCH_STATS, reporting is not limited to PE 0.

Fields:
  allocations       Successful bounce allocations while probing is enabled.
  waits             Allocations that entered the availability wait loop.
  limit_waits       Entries initially blocked by the local in-use limit.
  quota_waits       Entries initially denied by the optional node quota.
  drain_calls       Explicit CQ drains in this allocation wait loop.
  high_water        Maximum simultaneously in-use buffers seen after allocation.
  limit             Configured per-context maximum.
  wait_total_us     Total wall time in availability waits.
  wait_max_us       Longest availability wait.

limit_waits + quota_waits equals waits. Classification is at entry; a wait
can later encounter a different constraint. Time includes the loop's flush,
progress and counter-retirement work, but excludes allocation and memcpy.
Only wait entries read the clock; successful fast-path allocations update
occupancy/counts without timing. Internal helper CQ drains are not included in
drain_calls. Clock failures or backwards intervals are reported as errors.

An availability wait can be brief because completed buffers merely need CQ
processing. Counts/high-water alone do not establish a performance bottleneck;
use wait duration and controlled capacity/completion comparisons. These probes
do not establish the cause of slow NIC delivery.

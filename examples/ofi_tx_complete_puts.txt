Experimental full-width CXI transmit-complete puts
=================================================

Enable with SHMEM_OFI_CXI_TX_COMPLETE_PUTS=1. Default: off.
SHMEM_OFI_CXI_TX_COMPLETE_PUTS=0 overrides SMA_OFI_CXI_TX_COMPLETE_PUTS=1.
This is separate from SHMEM_OFI_CXI_CRAY_PUTS on the chunking branch;
that older switch does not enable this mode.

Network bulk puts:

* Every nonempty bulk put posts one full-width fi_writemsg with
  FI_TRANSMIT_COMPLETE, without FI_INJECT, FI_MORE or size-class chunking.
* Blocking puts wait for the context's issued-write counter before returning,
  making their source reusable. This is source completion, not remote delivery.
* Nonblocking puts do not wait during posting. Their source must remain valid
  and unchanged until completion through quiet or another completing operation.
* Sizes exceeding the provider's max_msg_size fail explicitly; the experimental
  path does not silently fragment them. Zero-length bulk puts post nothing.

Delivery and ordering:

* Quiet posts one zero-byte FI_FENCE | FI_DELIVERY_COMPLETE | FI_COMPLETION
  write per pending peer on the same endpoint as its payloads, then waits for
  the counter and identified CQ completion. FI_FENCE orders prior writes to
  that peer before the delivery marker; TX completion alone is not sufficient.
* Pending peers are sparse per context. One cached peer record needs no hash;
  a second peer promotes the active set to a hash. Overflow/hash storage is
  freed on drain and the cached record on destruction. Different sizes to the
  same peer coalesce without a size-change drain.
* Fence, dependent controls, shared-memory transitions and context destruction
  drain pending delivery. Scalar, signal and atomic controls retain strong
  completion. Blocking put-with-signal payloads remain delivery-complete.
  On-node puts retain the shared-memory transport.
* Context metadata uses the existing lock or a feature-only supplemental mutex.
  SHMEM_THREAD_MULTIPLE requires the provider's FI_THREAD_SAFE contract.

Admission and limits:

* Requires OFI with the CXI provider and a nonzero maximum message size.
  FI_CONTEXT/FI_CONTEXT2, FI_MR_LOCAL and HMEM are rejected explicitly.
* The disabled path preserves baseline endpoint flags and provider calls; it
  does not allocate peer state, initialize extra locks or issue delivery markers.
* FI_INJECT_COMPLETE is not used. This experiment tests transmit completion,
  not an earlier provider-specific source-completion implementation.
* Local provider mocks model source completion separately from remote delivery.
  They do not establish hardware visibility, high-PPN correctness or speedup.
  Production configure/build/install and compute jobs remain user-owned.
